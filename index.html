Speak French for your trip.

Privacy Policy Support

TravelFrench teaches the French you will actually use on your trip: ordering food, asking for directions, checking in. No grammar tables, just ready-made phrases and short exercises, with a plan that adapts to your travel date. There is no account to create and no server in the middle.

  • Listen, read, write and speak, with pronunciation feedback.
  • Smart review brings back the phrases you forget.
  • An offline phrasebook with big cards to show to someone.
  • Plus: private AI conversation practice, a camera translator for menus and signs, AI practice sentences and explanations of your mistakes. All of it runs on your iPhone.
  • Available in English and Italian.

Questions? See Support. To learn what TravelFrench does with your data (almost nothing), read the Privacy Policy.
