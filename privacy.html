Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Home Support
In short: TravelFrench has no accounts, no servers, no ads and no analytics. I do not collect any personal data. Your progress stays on your iPhone.

1. Who is responsible

TravelFrench is made by Matteo Saitta (contact: saithdevelopments@gmail.com).

2. What TravelFrench stores

The following is stored only on your iPhone, and I never receive it:

  • Your language choice, trip date and daily goal.
  • Your progress: completed lessons, stars, points, streak, badges, review schedule and starred phrases.
  • Your reminder settings.

A small summary of your progress (for example how many phrases are due for review) is shared with the TravelFrench widget on your Home Screen. This stays on your iPhone.

Deleting the app deletes this data. I cannot recover it.

3. What is not collected

TravelFrench does not collect your name, e-mail address, location, contacts, photos, advertising identifier or usage statistics, and it does not track you across other apps or websites. It contains no third-party code for analytics or advertising. If you choose to share crash reports with app developers, Apple may give me anonymous crash reports; I receive nothing otherwise.

4. Permissions

  • Microphone and Speech Recognition: used in the speaking exercises to check what you said. TravelFrench asks for on-device recognition whenever your iPhone supports it. If it does not, iOS may send the audio to Apple for processing under Apple’s own privacy terms. TravelFrench never stores your recordings.
  • Camera: used by the camera translator (Plus) to read printed text. Camera images are processed on your iPhone and are not saved or sent anywhere.
  • Notifications: used only for the daily practice reminder you choose. Reminders are scheduled on your iPhone.

You can turn each permission off at any time in the Settings app. The app still works without them.

5. AI and translation

The AI features (conversation, practice sentences and mistake explanations) use Apple Intelligence, which runs on your iPhone. What you type or say to the AI is not sent to me or to any server run by me. The camera translator uses Apple’s Translation framework on your iPhone. The first time you use a language, iOS may download it from Apple.

6. Purchases

Plus is an auto-renewing subscription handled entirely by Apple. I do not receive your name, payment details or Apple Account. Apple may give me anonymous sales totals.

7. Your choices

  • You can reset your progress in the app’s Settings.
  • You can delete everything by deleting the app.
  • You can cancel the subscription in Settings on your iPhone, under your name and Subscriptions.

Because I do not hold any of your personal data, there is nothing for me to access, correct or delete on your behalf.

8. Children

TravelFrench does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children. It has no accounts, no chat with other people and no links to other sites inside the lessons.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, I will update it here and change the date at the top.

10. Contact

Questions about privacy? Write to saithdevelopments@gmail.com.
